A Fledgling's Guide to the Living Dead

..According to the rules of Anne Rice's IWTV universe

Once a vampire turns you, you need to understand what you are and how to live sustainably.
What am I? What happened to me?
Allow me to explain..

  • What happened?
    A vampire has effectively drained you of just enough blood that you're incredibly weak and about to die.
  • What did you do?
    Your maker bit themselves open so you could drink up their blood to refill you
  • What does that mean?
    You have been turned into a vampire and must now get accustomed to your new body!!
  • Explain the process of becoming a fledgling, please!
    Basically, your body is dying while also coming back to life. You will at first feel drowsy, as if drunk, all loopy and falling around.
    Then, your body will feel the burn of the new blood coursing through, you're dying. You will feel like you're on fire.
    Then you're kinda gonna knock out, let's hope your maker is gonna look after you.
  • What is this feeling?
    So sudden and new?
    You're hungry, little fledgling.
    You crave blood.
  • What should I do?
    Fledglings are known for their at times extreme spells of hunger, trying to get any source of food they can, feeling starved.
